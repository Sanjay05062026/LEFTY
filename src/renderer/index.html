Lefty needs a few permissions to see your screen and point you in the right direction.

  • Screen capture Required

    See what's on your screen when you call Lefty.

  • Screen reading Required

    Read buttons and menus so Lefty can point at exactly the right spot.

  • Global shortcut Required

    Call Lefty from any app with Ctrl + Space.

  • Microphone Optional

    Hold Ctrl + Space and tell Lefty your goal, in your own language.

Settings

How Lefty helps

Learn: Lefty points and you click. Do it: Lefty clicks and types for you, and asks before anything risky.

Access

In Do it mode. Ask: a yes before every move. Auto: a yes before risky moves only. Full: no questions. Passwords and card numbers always stay yours.

Lefty's size

How big Lefty is drawn on your screen.

Lefty talks

Lefty reads his answers and each step out loud, in your language when Windows has a voice for it.

Privacy

Lefty sends something only when you ask him: your goal (typed or spoken), the names of the buttons on screen, and a screenshot of the screen you're on. It goes to the AI to work out the next step and is not kept. He never reads password boxes, and he clicks and types for you only in Do it mode.

Private apps

In these apps Lefty never takes a screenshot or reads your code. He works from the button names alone.

Often kept private:

Banking websites open inside your browser: to keep one private, add the browser while you use it.

Permissions

Screen capture, screen reading, the shortcut and the microphone.
